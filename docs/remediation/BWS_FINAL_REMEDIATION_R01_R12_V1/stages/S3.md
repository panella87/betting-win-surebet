## Rollback and interruption behavior

Stop new writes, classify the active tranche as `BLOCKED` unless an exact rollback completes, restore every changed byte/mode from the admitted preimage, remove only transaction-owned additions, preserve unrelated dirty content, and record ambiguity rather than inferring success. No later tranche may start from an unreceipted or mixed postimage.
